🚦 Road Accident Risk Analysis — India

Identifying high-risk time, weather, and location patterns using SQL, Python, and R (2018–2023 dataset, 3,000 records)

Project Overview

This project analyzes road accident data across Indian states to identify patterns in when, where, and under what conditions fatal accidents are most likely to occur. The goal is to generate insights that could support traffic police and policymakers in targeting interventions more effectively.

Key Takeaways

• Morning rush hour (7–8 AM) is the highest-risk time window for fatal accidents.
• Fatal accidents are spread fairly evenly across Indian states — a nationwide concern.
• State Highways have more fatal accidents than National Highways.
• Cyclists and bus-involved accidents have disproportionately high fatality counts.
• Weather condition does not show a statistically significant link to accident severity.
